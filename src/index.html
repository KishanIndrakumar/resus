Resus.ieED Multilingual Translate

Before you start

This is a demonstration prototype. It uses synthetic data only and must never replace a qualified interpreter, formal consent, or standard emergency assessment. No patient data is stored by this app — nothing you enter is saved, transmitted, or recoverable after you close or refresh it.

Translations are reproduced from the published British Red Cross / NHS Emergency Multilingual Phrasebook source material wherever possible, adapted for use in Ireland. Where the source material does not cover a question, this app does not invent a translation — it will say so.

For use by clinical staff only. Clinical and linguistic review is required before any live use.
